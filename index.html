About the Chapel

One family of faith, many traditions

The Chapel of Faith, FUTA is the interdenominational church of the Federal University of Technology, Akure. Students, staff and families from every Christian background worship here together, on campus, as one body.

Founded in 1984, the Chapel marked 40 years of worship in 2024. It is a place to pray between lectures, to find community far from home, and to grow in faith through every season of university life.

    Counselling

    You don’t have to carry it alone

    The Chaplaincy offers a listening ear and godly counsel for students and staff, in person or by phone.

    • Spiritual guidance & prayer
    • Academic stress & exam anxiety
    • Relationships, courtship & marriage
    • Grief, loss & family matters
    • Career & life direction
    Confidential

    What you share stays between you and your counsellor. You may book anonymously. If you are in danger right now, contact campus security or the FUTA Health Centre immediately.
